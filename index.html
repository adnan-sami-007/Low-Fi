Teacher / critique view

Why each element exists

Core question: What happens when we postpone satisfaction while waiting to become an imagined future self?

Two chairsContrast between the present self (already here) and the future self (expected but absent).
Press and holdThe visitor must sustain an action to experience duration instead of just reading about waiting.
Shifting milestonesGraduation, career, and success replace one another—the moving finish line.
Incomplete figureThe imagined future self remains undefined rather than becoming a guaranteed reward.
Final stillnessFocus turns back toward the present self; the ending is a question, not a command to stop striving.

Emotional arc: Curiosity → expectation → prolonged waiting → frustration → reflection.

Prototype note: This is a working low-fidelity study. The colors, furniture drawings, and effects are placeholders for the later visual-translation and Mid-Fi work.
